Know before you sign.

Hi neighbor. Before you sign a lease, type in the address and see what the city knows about the building: repairs, heat, pests, the landlord and more. It's free, and it all comes from public records.

    Try:

    Data comes live from NYC Open Data: HPD violations, complaints, registrations, legal unit counts, litigation, bedbug filings, vacate orders, the Alternative Enforcement Program and the Certification of No Harassment list; DOB complaints, violations, ECB violations, permits, after-hours variances and boiler inspections; ACRIS property records; DOHMH rodent inspections; 311 service requests; FloodNet sensors, the 2020s floodplain, the Sandy inundation zone and hurricane evacuation zones; the Speculation Watch List; DOI marshal evictions; DCP PLUTO; and NYC Planning's GeoSearch.

    Comparisons use a random sample of 220 NYC buildings with three or more apartments (90 for noise and rats), drawn in October 2026. “The block” means everything within 150 meters, about a block in each direction.

    HPD records mainly cover rental buildings with three or more units. Co-ops, condos and small homes may show little or nothing. A clean record is a good sign, not a guarantee.
